to Code

It started with a simple question — why does this work? Growing up in Gujarat, I became fascinated by how software solves real problems. That curiosity became an obsession, and that obsession became a career path.

I'm currently in my final year of Diploma in Computer Engineering at K.D. Polytechnic, Patan. What started as academic requirements quickly became passion projects — building full-stack applications, training AI models, and deploying real products.

I'm drawn to the intersection of AI and practical software development. Not AI for buzzwords — AI that genuinely makes applications smarter. My final year project, an ATS Resume Analyzer, captures exactly that: real NLP solving a real problem.

When I'm not coding, I'm exploring new frameworks, digging into IoT, and always asking: how can this be built better?
